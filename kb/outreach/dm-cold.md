# Cold Outreach DM

## When to use
No prior relationship. Reaching out to a hiring manager, recruiter, or employee at a target company where you have no shared history, no mutual connection, and no prior interaction.

## Calibrated ask
Ask for a low-friction next step: "Are you open to a brief look at my resume?" or "Happy to share more context if useful." Do not ask for a call as the first ask. Do not ask for a referral. The ask must be easy to say yes or no to in under 10 seconds.

## Template

```
Hi [Name],

[1-sentence hook: what caught your attention about the company or role -- specific, not generic. Name the product problem they are solving.]

[1--2 sentences: the most directly applicable credential from your background -- what you built, a result you shipped. Match the product problem in the hook.]

[1-sentence low-friction ask.]

[Name]
linkedin.com/in/michael-korenevsky
```

---

## Structural pattern (from Windward cover letter)

The Windward cover letter opens with a domain-matching move: "Both products I've owned at Oqton deal with spatial data." It names the match first, then explains the mechanics, then closes with a domain gap acknowledgment. In DM format this compresses to: hook (their problem), credential (your matching product), ask.

The Oriient cover letter opens with a direct mirror: "[Company] turns [X] into [Y]. At Oqton, I built the same thing." This works in a DM because it lands the relevance before the reader asks "why are you messaging me?"

The Medison cover letter opens with the role requirement, then immediately states "is what I've done twice." In a cold DM, this compresses to: what they need + one sentence of proof.

Pattern: **mirror their product problem, name your matching product, make one ask.**

## Worked examples

### Example 1: Spatial / data product (adapted from Windward cover letter)

```
Hi [Name],

I saw your team is working on [maritime spatial intelligence / platform-level data pipelines] at Windward.

Both products I've owned at Oqton deal with spatial data: AMVero flags 3D printing defects by their location and shape across multi-vendor machines, and the Simulation Suite maps where and how much a part will deform before it's printed. The hard part in both was the same: multi-source data with uneven quality, and calling what's good enough to surface to the customer.

Happy to share my resume if useful.

Michael Korenevsky
linkedin.com/in/michael-korenevsky
```

### Example 2: AI product / 0-to-1 (adapted from Oriient cover letter)

```
Hi [Name],

[Company] turns indoor positioning data into operational intelligence. At Oqton, I built the same thing for manufacturing: raw sensor data from physical machines, processed by AI, surfaced as a clear decision for a non-technical operator.

I took that AI capability from model to product: model accuracy, UX, customer discovery, and GTM. We signed 5 enterprise accounts within 5 months, including Thales and Baker Hughes.

Are you open to a brief look at my profile?

Michael Korenevsky
linkedin.com/in/michael-korenevsky
```

### Example 3: Recruiter outreach

```
Hi [Name],

I saw you're managing the search for the [Role Title] role at [Company].

I have [3+ / 4+] years of PM ownership shipping AI-powered products to enterprise clients in [aerospace, defense, and medical / regulated manufacturing], most recently building an AI monitoring platform from zero to 5 enterprise customers in 5 months at Oqton.

Are you open to a brief look at my resume to see if it's a fit?

Michael Korenevsky
linkedin.com/in/michael-korenevsky
```
