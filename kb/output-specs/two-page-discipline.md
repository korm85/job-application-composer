# Two-Page Discipline

## Rule

CVs must fit exactly two pages after applying the Google Docs formatting defaults in `cv-format.md`.

**Do NOT silently cut content to make a CV fit.**

## When overflow is detected

1. STOP. Do not produce a final output.
2. Report the overflow: state the page count and which section(s) are over.
3. Diagnose the cause: too many bullets? Summary too long? QA roles not compressed?
4. Propose 2–4 ranked options with tradeoffs. Example format:

```
The CV is currently 3 pages. Here are 4 options to bring it to 2:

Option A (recommended): Remove 1 Oqton PM bullet (the weakest archetype match for this JD).
  Trade-off: Loses [specific claim]. Can recover from Bench for other applications.

Option B: Compress the summary from 3 lines to 2.
  Trade-off: Loses [specific framing]. Retain in Bench as a summary candidate.

Option C: Move [specific bullet] to Bench — it appeared only in this variant and does not match the JD archetype.
  Trade-off: [impact]

Option D: Reduce both QA role bullets (currently 1 each) to a single combined QA line.
  Trade-off: Loses specificity on role progression. Acceptable if the target is not evaluating QA depth.
```

5. Wait for user approval before any cut.
6. After approved cuts: append to the variant's `change-log` section.

## What requires approval (anything that removes or rewrites a claim)

- Removing a bullet
- Shortening a bullet by removing a claim, metric, or named technology
- Removing a summary line
- Reordering sections to save space

## What does NOT require approval (mechanical compression)

- Removing trailing whitespace or extra blank lines
- Standardizing punctuation (period vs. no period at end of bullets)
- Shortening date format (e.g., "February 2025" → "Feb 2025")

## Hard prohibitions

- No font size below 11pt body
- No margin reduction below 0.5"
- No line spacing below single
- Never shrink to fit without user approval on every cut
