# Target Roles — Archetypes and Tone Shifts

## How to use
Identify closest archetype from the JD. Confirm with user if ambiguous. Select bullets by tag. Apply tone shift.

---

## PLG / SaaS PM [PLG]
**When:** SaaS companies, product-led growth, self-serve, usage analytics, rapid iteration, B2C-influenced B2B.
**Emphasize:** Customer feedback loops, iteration speed, usage signal, data-driven decisions, onboarding simplification, pricing model innovation, beta validation.
**Key bullets/themes:** Visual scripting prioritized from customer feedback (Simulation Suite), beta validation with enterprise customers, pricing model redesign (flat-to-credit), internal RAG portal as self-serve mindset, AMVero customer signing.
**De-emphasize:** Protocol depth (OPC-UA/MQTT), defense/regulatory framing, low-level engineering specs.
**Bullet tags:** `[PLG]`

---

## Agentic AI PM [Agentic]
**When:** Companies building AI agents, LLM products, automation workflows, AI-native tooling, GenAI platforms.
**Emphasize:** LLM workflows, data curation (Label Studio), evals, RAG portal (Ollama), AMVero AI defect detection, autonomous decision engine, personal agentic tooling (OpenClaw, Claude Code, Figma Make), AI model performance iteration.
**Key bullets/themes:** AMVero 90% accuracy decision engine, Label Studio data curation method, internal RAG pipeline on Ollama, Figma Make and Claude Code prototyping workflow.
**De-emphasize:** QA history, non-AI PM work, hardware-framing without AI connection.
**Bullet tags:** `[Agentic]`

---

## Enterprise PM [Enterprise]
**When:** B2B SaaS, enterprise sales, regulated industries, multi-stakeholder products, long sales cycles, aerospace/defense/medical/energy verticals.
**Emphasize:** Cross-functional collaboration, integration complexity, regulated industries (ITAR, SOC 2), auditability, data residency, role-based access, tier-1 customer names (Thales, Baker Hughes), whitepaper publishing, structured discovery with on-site operators, compliance as a hard constraint from day one.
**Key bullets/themes:** AMVero customer signing (5 accounts in 5 months, named), ITAR/SOC 2 compliance as feature, whitepapers after beta, cross-functional team of 11, enterprise discovery process.
**De-emphasize:** PLG/self-serve framing, internal tool building, rapid-prototype developer workflows (unless tied to cost/time reduction at enterprise scale).
**Bullet tags:** `[Enterprise]`

---

## Technical Product Owner [TechPO]
**When:** IoT, industrial, hardware-adjacent, engineering-heavy teams, requirements-lifecycle-focused roles, companies building on physical infrastructure.
**Emphasize:** OPC-UA, MQTT, physics-based simulation, cross-machine/cross-vendor integration, engineering collaboration, epics and user story lifecycle, B.Sc. Mechanical Engineering, QA background as release-gate discipline.
**Key bullets/themes:** OPC-UA/MQTT data integration requirements, thermo-mechanical/thermal simulation modules, cross-vendor machine model training, requirements lifecycle ownership, QA background for edge-case rigor.
**De-emphasize:** PLG framing, general PM soft skills, pricing/GTM strategy.
**Bullet tags:** `[TechPO]`
