# Voice and Writing Rules

## Core principles
- Plain, specific, defensible language
- Every bullet must be defensible in an interview
- No vague claims without concrete grounding
- No metric or number may appear in any outbound material unless it exists in facts.md and is marked CONFIRMED, or is explicitly approved by the user

## Punctuation and style
- No em dashes — use colons or commas
- Contact line separator: `  •  ` (use consistently; do not mix with ` | `)
- LinkedIn URL: short form only: `linkedin.com/in/michael-korenevsky` (no `http://` or `www.`)

## Buzzword prohibitions
- synergistic, leveraged, spearheaded, best-in-class, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, disrupted, thought leader
- "measurable impact on customer efficiency" (explicitly banned phrase; replace with a specific figure from facts.md)
- "developer velocity" (use "feature lead time" or specific hours saved)
- "strategic alignment" (use "roadmap prioritization" or "discovery alignment")
- "actionable insights" (specify what the insights produced)

## Bullet discipline
- Max 6 bullets per role for general CVs
- Max 7 bullets for technical-depth variants (NICE-style) — each independently defensible
- QA roles (3D Systems, Cimatron): 1 bullet each; all others to the Bench
- No bullet added just to match a JD — adjust existing bullets only
- No metric unless confirmed in facts.md
- Do not echo JD language verbatim unless it genuinely matches experience

## Summary rules
- 2–3 lines only
- No QA-to-PM narrative in summary
- No "Senior" in summary or tagline
- "Senior" in Experience section header only, for the most recent Oqton role

## Structure rules
- No "Side Projects" section — the internal RAG portal is an Oqton bullet under the Senior PM role
- Section order: Summary → Experience → Education → Skills (Skills only if adding signal)
- Heading hierarchy: H1 name, H2 sections, H3 roles
- No horizontal rules, no color, no icons, no photo

## Output format
- .md by default; .docx only when explicitly requested

## Tailoring limits
- Hard ceiling: 6 bullets general, 7 technical-depth only
- Do not add a new bullet solely to match a JD keyword; rephrase or reorder existing bullets

## Signal guidance
- Signal engineering depth where relevant ("physics-based", "cross-vendor", "multi-machine")
- Signal AI hands-on work where relevant ("Label Studio", "Ollama", "RAG pipeline", "autonomous decision engine")
